Why UV-C?

Visible cleaning is important.
Invisible protection matters.

Our professional UV-C technology helps reduce viruses, bacteria, mold and other pathogens on surfaces and in the air that cleaning alone can't reach.

UV-C treatment supplements, but does not replace, manual cleaning. Treatment is limited to areas receiving adequate UV-C exposure. Spaces must be vacant during operation. Effectiveness depends on dose, distance, exposure time, organism and surface conditions.

Our Simple 4-Step Process

How a Healthy Handoff works

1

Clean

Your space is professionally cleaned and prepped. Healthy Handoff always follows conventional cleaning — never replaces it.

2

Treat

We use commercial-grade UV-C technology to treat surfaces and air in each room. The space must be vacant during operation.

3

Document

You receive a Healthy Handoff Treatment Certificate with date, time and technician details — for your records and peace of mind.

4

Return

The space is ready — keys change hands with a documented final step behind them.
